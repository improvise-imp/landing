Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your training mechanics.

What exactly is Improvise?
Improvise is a psychological survival training app designed to help users sharpen their decision-making skills and instincts under intense life pressure.
How does Real Life Mode work?
This feature is designed for active, real-world problems. By typing out the stressful situation you are currently facing, the app provides a structured analysis to help you clear your head and regain clarity instantly.
Will the training get repetitive?
No. The app features Dynamic Drills, which provide fresh, standard scenarios to ensure your daily training always feels new.
What happens in an Elite Scenario?
These are high-stakes simulations built specifically to test your composure when placed under intense pressure. You are presented with multiple choices and a strict 25-second countdown timer to lock in your decision. This format removes the chance to hesitate, effectively training your response before real pressure tests it.
